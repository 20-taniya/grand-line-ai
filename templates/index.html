⚔️ Meet Your Crew ⚔️

Every legend has a story. Meet the crew that will sail beside you across every question and every adventure.

👒 Monkey D. Luffy

Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates

Monkey D. Luffy is the cheerful and fearless captain of the Straw Hat Pirates, whose greatest dream is to find the legendary One Piece and become the Pirate King. Inspired by the legendary pirate Shanks during his childhood, Luffy grew up dreaming of a life at sea and eventually set out on his own adventure after gaining rubber-like abilities from a Devil Fruit. Along his journey, he gathered a crew he considers his family and faced countless enemies and impossible challenges. Despite his carefree and sometimes reckless nature, Luffy has an unbreakable will and will never abandon someone he cares about. His journey across the Grand Line is driven by his love for freedom, adventure, and the people who sail beside him.

"I'll become the King of the Pirates!"

⚔️ Roronoa Zoro

Combatant • Master Swordsman

Roronoa Zoro is the powerful swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of Luffy's most loyal companions. From a young age, Zoro trained relentlessly at a dojo and dreamed of becoming the world's greatest swordsman after making a promise to his childhood rival and friend, Kuina. After her tragic death, Zoro carried her dream with him and continued his journey as a bounty hunter before meeting Luffy. Impressed by Luffy's determination, he became the first member of the Straw Hat crew and dedicated himself to helping his captain become Pirate King while pursuing his own ambition. Calm, stubborn, and fiercely determined, Zoro is willing to sacrifice everything for his crew and his promise.

"Nothing happened."

🧭 Nami

Navigator • Cartographer

Nami is the brilliant navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates, gifted with an exceptional understanding of weather, maps, and the unpredictable seas of the Grand Line. She was adopted by Bell-mère and grew up in Cocoyasi Village alongside Nojiko, dreaming of one day drawing a complete map of the world. Her peaceful childhood was shattered when the Arlong Pirates took control of her village and forced Nami to work for them as a navigator and cartographer. Although she initially joined the Straw Hats for her own reasons, Luffy and the crew eventually helped free her village from Arlong's control. Since then, Nami has become an essential member of the crew, using her intelligence, courage, and navigation skills to guide them toward their dreams.

"I'll guide you to the best route."

👨‍🍳 Vinsmoke Sanji

Cook • Black Leg Fighter

Vinsmoke Sanji is the talented chef and skilled fighter of the Straw Hat Pirates, known for his powerful kicks, refined manners, and dream of finding the legendary All Blue. Born into the powerful Vinsmoke family, Sanji was treated differently because of his kindness and love for cooking, eventually leaving his family behind. His life changed when he met the pirate chef Zeff after a shipwreck, and the two survived together under extreme circumstances. Zeff became Sanji's mentor and taught him both cooking and the importance of never wasting food. Sanji later worked at the Baratie restaurant before meeting Luffy, who convinced him to join the Straw Hats and pursue his dream. Beneath his dramatic and flirtatious personality is a deeply compassionate man who will always put his friends and their safety before himself.

"A hungry person deserves a great meal."
